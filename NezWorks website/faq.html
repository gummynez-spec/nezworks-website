Getting started

01 What is NezWorks? →

NezWorks is an orbit-driven creative platform — connecting businesses and brands with professional freelance creators across Logo, Banner, Poster, Video, Graphic Design and Content. Everything is handled in one place, end to end.

02 How do I start a project? →

Just contact the creator of the work you like — directly through the website. Share what you need (a logo, a video edit, a banner…) and the creator takes it from there. No waiting around for quotes.

03 What services do you offer? →

Six main categories: Logo (mark, wordmark, identity), Banner (web, ads, social headers), Poster (events, promos, print), Video (editing, visual storytelling), Graphic Design (brand graphics) and Content (copy, social posts, articles). New categories are added regularly.

04 Can I work with you on an ongoing basis? →

Yes — we support recurring work such as regular content, long-term brand systems, and campaign-by-campaign projects. For monthly or quarterly packages, just let us know through the Contact page.

Pricing & Payment

05 How much does a project cost? →

Every piece of work shows a clear starting price — from 500 THB. You know the budget in advance, with no waiting for a quote. The exact price is confirmed before any work begins.

06 How do I pay? Is it safe? →

Payment is made against the invoice your creator issues. Once you receive it, attach your payment slip to approve — the invoice then becomes your receipt. Simple, traceable and protected on both sides. No money is ever transferred off-platform.

07 Are there any hidden fees? →

None. Prices are shown upfront — no surprise charges for clients, and freelancer fees are always clear before starting. The only optional cost is our 199 THB featured fee, which lets a creator place their work on the front page so clients and visitors see it first.

Delivery

08 How long until I get my work? →

You get a first reply within 10 hours. Actual delivery time depends on the size and complexity of the project — the creator sets a clear timeline before work starts.

09 What if I don't like the result? →

Every project follows the creator's own review process, with a minimum of one free revision round. You review the work before finalising — so nothing is delivered without your sign-off.

10 What files do I get after the work is done? →

All final files: the usable work files, source files, and launch support if needed. Everything is reviewed before release, and ownership transfers to you on delivery.

For freelancers

11 How do I apply to become a freelancer? →

Head to the Career page and apply. NezWorks brings you work, matches you with projects, handles communication and takes care of payments — you focus purely on creating.

12 How do freelancers get paid? Any fees? →

Once the client approves, you're paid through your invoice and the payment slip. A transparent platform fee applies based on your tier — always disclosed before you start. Want your work seen first? The 199 THB featured fee puts it on the front page.
