Terms for freelancers

To keep work smooth and fair for both sides, please read and accept before getting started.

Fees
  • 0–20,000 THB → 8%
  • 20,001–100,000 THB → 7%
  • 100,001–300,000 THB → 6%
  • 300,001 THB and up → 5%

No sign-up or listing fees — you pay only when work is delivered.

Payments

Payments are processed on-platform, with fees deducted per the rate above before funds are transferred to you. This protects both sides.

Work & Delivery

Deliver per the details, price, and timeline you agreed on. If anything changes, agree through the NezWorks system so there is a clear record.

Cancellation & Disputes

If you can't continue a project, let the client and NezWorks know so we can find the right solution. In a dispute, NezWorks may ask both sides for evidence to reach a fair decision.

Work & Others' Rights

Deliver only work you own or have the right to use. Don't infringe copyright or anyone else's rights.

Reviews

After a project, both sides may rate and review honestly. No fake reviews or tampering with scores.

Fair Use

Use the platform honestly — no fraud, no false information, no bypassing the system or its fees. If risky behavior is found, NezWorks may temporarily suspend your account.

Personal Data

We collect and use only the data needed to provide the service, verify identity, process payments, and keep the platform safe — per our privacy policy.
