THE TRAJECTORY

Five steps. Zero blind spots.

01

Contact a creator you like

Browse the work on the site and message the creator directly — through the website, no third parties. Tell them what you want: a logo, a poster, a video edit.

02

Agree, then we create

The creator confirms the exact price and timeline — starting from 500 THB, shown up front. Then the work starts, with you in the loop at every milestone.

03

Reviews follow each creator's process — always including at least one free revision round. Nothing ships without your nod.

Final files, source assets and launch support land in your dashboard. You keep full rights once it's delivered to you.

The creator sends an invoice. You attach your payment slip to approve, and the invoice becomes your receipt. Clear, traceable, protected for both sides.
